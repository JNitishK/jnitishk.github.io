HI, HELLO, VANAKKAM!

I'm Nitish 👋, an Industrial & Operations Engineering graduate from the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, with a passion for connecting engineering, business, and technology to solve complex operational challenges.is interested in Business Operations and Supply Chain Management.

Currently, I work with General Motors in the Global Purchasing & Supply Chain organization, focusing on supplier industrialization, automation maturity, and sourcing analytics. My work involves evaluating supplier automation capabilities and supporting data-driven decision-making across manufacturing supply chains.

My journey began at NIT Trichy, India, where I earned my Bachelor's in Production Engineering. Since then, I've gained experience across manufacturing operations, business intelligence, supply chain analytics, and continuous improvement, working at the intersection of technical problem-solving and business strategy.

Beyond the numbers and processes, I'm naturally curious, enjoy creative problem-solving, and believe that the best solutions emerge when analytical thinking meets a fresh perspective. I'm always looking to learn, collaborate, and make a meaningful impact.
